01 · A letter from Momo

Dear Polite 💌

Happy birthday, Sis.

It is honestly funny how people can enter your life in such a simple way and somehow become important without you even noticing exactly when it happened. We just decided that you were my sister and I was your brother — and somehow, here we are. You calling me Momo, me calling you Sis, and telling each other things that we might not tell everyone else.

Since last year, you have become someone I genuinely enjoy talking to. We vent. We complain. We share our problems, the strange events happening in our lives, and all the random things in between. There is something I really value about the fact that we do not feel like we have to hide everything from each other.

And if there is one thing I appreciate most about you, it is your honesty. Real honesty is rare. Being able to trust someone to tell you the truth — even when life is messy — means more than people realise.

Also, let's not forget your contribution to my personal investigation department. 😭 You actually followed a certain person's movements and told me what was happening. And unfortunately for my heart at the time, the information was very effective. 💀😂 But genuinely, your honesty helped me see things clearly and helped me move forward.

You are kind. You are loyal. And yes... you are a little short-tempered. 😭😂 But that is Polite. That is Sis. That is you.

On your sixteenth birthday, I hope you remember that you matter to the people who genuinely know you. I hope this new year of your life brings you growth, peace, laughter, beautiful memories, and people who appreciate your heart properly.

Thank you for being my sister. Thank you for listening when I needed someone. Thank you for trusting me with your own problems. Thank you for being honest with me.

And thank you for simply being Polite.

Happy 16th, Sis. 💗
— Your brother, Momo
